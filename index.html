ABOUT AVERY

SYS_ANALYSIS // ACADEMIC PROFILE

Investigating the intersection of Law, Data, & Behavior

I am a passionate undergraduate student at Niagara University pursuing a degree in Criminal Justice and Criminology. My academic trajectory centers on analyzing criminological patterns, forensic methodology, and behavioral psychology to understand both the systemic and individual causes of criminal conduct.

Combining traditional criminal justice theory with contemporary analytical technologies, my work focuses on quantitative crime mapping, criminal profiling frameworks, and evidence-based correctional policies. I am dedicated to contributing toward modern, data-informed justice solutions.
